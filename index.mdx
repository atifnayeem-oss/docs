---
title: "What is Ringup"
description: "Identity and payments for voice agents. Returning callers are recognized by phone number and pay with their saved card in one sentence."
---

Ringup is a **remembered checkout** for phone calls. A caller who has paid once is recognized
by phone number on every call after and pays again in one sentence: no card read aloud, no
app, nothing to repeat.

For you it is a **payment orchestration engine**. You integrate Ringup once, never each
payment processor: the same API charges whichever processor a merchant runs. Your agent keeps
the conversation; Ringup runs the money. No payment prompt to write, nothing to configure: the
payment step inherits everything from your agent.

Your merchants **keep the payment setup they already have**. Charges settle on each merchant's
own processor, at their rates, in their dashboard, and refunds, disputes, chargebacks, and
reporting all run exactly where they run today.

## What Ringup never does

- **Never reads or stores card numbers.** Card entry happens once, on a Ringup-hosted page.
  The number never appears in audio, transcripts, or your servers, so no PCI scope lands on
  you.
- **Never creates a new processor account.** Charges settle on the merchant's existing
  payment processor.
- **Never hard-codes payment behavior into your agent.** How payment works is a merchant
  setting served by Ringup at call time; merchants change it with zero redeploys.
